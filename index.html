Nonequilibrium Quantum Matter Workshop

Quantum simulation, monitoring and emergent order

June 8–9, 2026 Tübingen, Germany

Register Now!

Workshop format

The workshop will feature comprehensive lectures by invited speakers covering many aspects of open quantum systems, from theoretical concepts to their implementation in quantum technologies.
The event is aimed at master's and PhD students, as well as early career researchers in quantum optics, statistical mechanics and condensed matter physics.
Registration is free of charge, but required for attendance, and we will cover coffee breaks, lunches and the conference dinner.
We cannot provide financial support for travel and accommodation, but we will be happy to assist with suggestions. For any questions regarding the workshop format and registration, please contact us at neqm@itp.uni-tuebingen.de.

Topics

  • Non-Hermitian topology
  • Monitoring and post-selection
  • Criticality and phase transitions
  • Nonequilibrium long-range order
  • Quantum simulation of many-body physics
